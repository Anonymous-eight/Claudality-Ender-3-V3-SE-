# MASTER_FIGURE_LIST

## Purpose

This document is the official master list of all planned illustrations for the **Claudality Open-Source DIY Fabrication Guide**.

It serves as the authoritative reference for contributors, reviewers, and future editions of the book.

## Instructions for Contributors

- Do not modify figure numbers.
- Do not rename figure titles.
- Submit illustrations matching the figure title as accurately as possible.
- Only submit original work or legally reusable images in accordance with the repository copyright policy.
- If multiple illustrations are available, prioritize technical accuracy and publishing quality.
